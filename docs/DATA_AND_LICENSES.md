## FlyWire connectome (not included)

The brain is wired from the **FlyWire whole-brain connectome, FAFB materialization v783**, released
under **CC-BY 4.0** by the FlyWire Consortium. Download instructions are in
[`../pi/README.md`](../pi/README.md). If you publish anything built on it, cite:

* Dorkenwald, S. et al. *Neuronal wiring diagram of an adult brain.* Nature 634, 124–138 (2024).
* Schlegel, P. et al. *Whole-brain annotation and multi-connectome cell typing of Drosophila.*
  Nature 634, 139–152 (2024).

The stream's brain visual places each neuron at the centroid of its synapses from the public
synapse table (`flywire_synapses_783`, Zenodo), computed once on the Pi.
